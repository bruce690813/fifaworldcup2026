Wikidata stadium data

The league stadium names, Wikidata entity identifiers and geographic
coordinates used by the league home-stadium map are derived from Wikidata.

Source: https://www.wikidata.org/
License: Creative Commons CC0 1.0 Universal
License URL: https://creativecommons.org/publicdomain/zero/1.0/

The OpenStreetMap basemap is loaded at runtime and displays the attribution
required by the OpenStreetMap contributors license:
https://www.openstreetmap.org/copyright

The Zhong-Yi Elementary School coordinate used for the ZYES youth-team map
was resolved from the school official address through OpenStreetMap Nominatim.
OpenStreetMap data is available under the Open Data Commons Open Database
License (ODbL).
